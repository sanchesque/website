Privacy

This website has no forms or sign-ups and does not ask for your personal data.

  • If you email us, we use your address and what you tell us only to reply and, where relevant, to prepare a proposal. We don't share it with anyone and we delete it once it is no longer needed.
  • Visit statistics. We use GoatCounter, which counts visits without cookies or personal data: we only see aggregated figures (page views, country, browser type and where the visit came from).
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, technically logs the IP address of requests for security.
  • Fonts and assets are served from this website itself: your browser does not connect to Google or any other service to display it.

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data by writing to hello@sanchesque.com. If you think we have not handled it properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Cookies

We don't use cookies. The only thing this website stores in your browser is the language you choose with the ES/EN button, so it can show it to you next time. It stays on your device and you can clear it from your browser.
