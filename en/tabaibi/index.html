Tabaibi, your hotel's
digital concierge.

An assistant that answers your guests instantly, at any hour and in their language, using your hotel's verified information. When it isn't sure, it doesn't make things up: it hands over to reception.

Get a demo How it works

How it works.

01

Your information, verified

We start from your website and documents to build your hotel's FAQ. Your team reviews and approves every answer before it goes live.

02

Instant answers

Guests write the way they talk, and Tabaibi understands what they mean even when they phrase it differently, replying in Spanish or English.

03

Unsure? It hands over

When it isn't confident, it says so and sends the guest to reception. That question is logged so the assistant learns and improves over time.

What your guests ask.

The same questions reception answers dozens of times a day. For example:

  • “What time is breakfast?”
  • “How late is the pool open?”
  • “Do you have beach towels?”
  • “Can I leave my luggage after check-out?”
  • “What's the wifi password?”
  • “How do I get to the airport?”
  • «¿Hay un gimnasio cerca?»
  • «¿Puedo reservar mesa para cenar esta noche?»

Who it's for.

Places that want to look after guests better

  • Hotels and aparthotels
  • Holiday apartments and rentals
  • Rural houses and guesthouses
  • Resorts with several buildings or a shared reception

With your data under your control

  • Runs on a low-cost device installed at your property, or hosted in the cloud if you prefer.
  • Uses only information your team has approved.
  • Stores no personal data about your guests.
  • Your chat, your look: your colours, your logo and your name.

Want to see it at your hotel?

Tabaibi is in its pilot phase. If you run a property, we'll build a demo from your website so you can try it with real questions.
