Request stickers¶

Missing a theme? Know someone who draws really well? The Request tab is for that. We read everything.

How to send a request¶

  1. Open the "Request" tab.
  2. Choose "Suggest a theme" or "Recommend a creator".
  3. Write your idea (1 to 500 characters). If you recommend a creator, tell us who draws and where we can see their work.
  4. Tap Send request.

You will see "Request sent. Thank you".

What is sent¶

Only the text you write, the type of request, the app language, whether you use Android or iPhone, and a time. We do not ask for your name, email or phone, and you do not need an account. The request is anonymous.

Please do not put personal data in the text. Only the studio reads it, never other users.

Limits¶

  • You can send one request a day. If you already sent one, the app tells you how long is left ("You can send another in … h").
  • The text can be up to 500 characters.
  • If sending fails (for example, no connection), your text is kept so you can try again.

What happens next¶

Requests are reviewed about once a month, and new packs come out of that review, also in monthly batches. A request can therefore take up to a month to be read.

We do not reply to requests. We have no contact details for you, so we cannot tell you that your idea was picked. If a pack comes out of your idea, you will see it in "Explore".
