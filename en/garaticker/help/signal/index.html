Stickers on Signal¶

Signal has no button like WhatsApp's. Garaticker packs for Signal are published separately, by hand, so they may come out a little later than on other platforms.

How to add a pack¶

Signal saves sticker packs from a pack link. With the link on your phone:

  1. Open the pack link.
  2. Signal shows the pack. Tap the button to install it.
  3. In a chat, tap the sticker icon and find the pack in your list.

The exact steps can change with Signal versions. If something does not match, see Signal's own help (support.signal.org, "Stickers" article).

What is special about it¶

  • One emoji per sticker. Signal supports only one emoji for each sticker, so on Signal you will see just one, even if the sticker has several on other platforms.
  • Packs cannot be edited. Once a pack is published on Signal, it cannot be changed or deleted. If something needs fixing, a new pack is published.
  • Animated. Packs with an animated version have their stickers animated on Signal.

Common problems¶

I cannot find the pack in Signal. Check that you opened the pack link and tapped the button to install it. Update Signal if it does not work.

A pack I saw is not on Signal. Not every pack is published on every platform. Try WhatsApp or Telegram.
