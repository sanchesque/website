Garaticker · Help¶

Garaticker is a free app of Canarian sticker packs, made by artists from the islands. It comes from Sanchesque Studios. You can get it on Android and on iPhone (on iPhone it includes an iMessage extension).

Pick a pack, tap a button and use it in your chats. No account, no ads, no purchases.

Where you can use them¶

Platform How
WhatsApp From the app, with the "Add to WhatsApp" button
Telegram From the app, with the "Open in Telegram" button
Signal Packs published separately, with one emoji per sticker
iMessage iPhone only, with the Garaticker extension in Messages

Animated packs are also on GIPHY. Search for Garaticker stickers in apps that use GIPHY. More in the FAQ.

More help¶

  • Missing some stickers? Ask for them from the "Request" tab.
  • FAQ: free, offline, removing packs, artists and more.

Contact¶

Something not working, or a question? Write to us at hello@sanchesque.com. Tell us which phone you have, the app version and what happened.
