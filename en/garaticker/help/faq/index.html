FAQ¶

Is Garaticker free?¶

Yes. There are no purchases, paid packs or ads.

Do I need an account?¶

No. There is no sign-up and we do not ask for your name, email or phone.

Which platforms can I use the stickers on?¶

On WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal and iMessage (iPhone only). The Android app does not include iMessage, because it does not exist on Android.

What is GIPHY and where are the stickers there?¶

GIPHY is a search engine for GIFs and stickers used by many apps. Garaticker's animated stickers are there. Search for "Garaticker" in apps that use GIPHY. Static packs are not published on GIPHY.

Does it work offline?¶

Packs you already downloaded stay in the app. Offline, the catalog does not update and the app tells you: "Offline: the catalog was not updated. Your downloaded packs are still here." To download a new pack you need a connection.

How do I save my favorite stickers?¶

Press and hold a sticker and choose "Add to favorites". You will find them in the "Favorites" tab. To remove one, choose "Remove from favorites".

Can I share a single sticker?¶

Yes. Open the sticker and tap "Share" to send it to any app. On iPhone you can also tap "Copy".

How do I remove a pack?¶

Packs added to WhatsApp, Telegram or Signal are removed from those apps, in your sticker list. In Garaticker you do not need to remove anything.

Who makes the stickers?¶

Artists from the islands. Garaticker brings together packs by Sanchesque Studios and by guest artists. In the "Artists" tab you can see who is who, with their links and packs. Each pack carries its author's name.

Can I change the language?¶

The app is in Spanish and English. Change it in "Settings", under "Language", which opens your phone's language setting.

Can I ask for new stickers?¶

Yes, from the "Request" tab. See how it works.

How do I report a problem?¶

Write to us at hello@sanchesque.com with your phone, the app version and what happened. First, check the page for your platform: WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal or iMessage.

What data do you keep?¶

Very little. You do not need an account: the app does not ask for your name, email or phone number. There are no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics. Your favourites and settings stay on your phone. If you send a request, see what is stored.
