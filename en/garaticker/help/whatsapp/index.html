Add stickers to WhatsApp¶

This is the easiest one: one button per pack.

Step by step¶

  1. Open Garaticker and go into a pack from the "Explore" tab.
  2. If the pack has an animated version, choose "Static" or "Animated" above the stickers. WhatsApp does not mix the two in one pack.
  3. Tap Add to WhatsApp. The button shows progress while the pack downloads.
  4. WhatsApp asks you to confirm. Accept.
  5. When it all went well, the button changes to "Added to WhatsApp".
  6. Open any WhatsApp chat, tap the emoji icon and go to the sticker tab. Your pack is there.

The process is the same on Android and iPhone. On Android, the confirmation dialog is WhatsApp's own.

Before you start¶

  • WhatsApp must be installed on your phone and up to date.
  • You need a connection to download the pack the first time.

Common problems¶

It says "WhatsApp not found". The app cannot detect WhatsApp. Install it from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone), or use Telegram.

It says "The pack could not be added. Try again." Check your connection and tap the button again. If it keeps failing, update WhatsApp and Garaticker.

Stickers do not show up in WhatsApp. Close WhatsApp and open it again. If the pack still does not show up, remove it in WhatsApp and add it again from Garaticker.

I want both an animated and a static pack. They are two separate packs in WhatsApp. Add each one on its own from the pack screen.

I want to remove a pack. You do it from WhatsApp, in your sticker list. See also the FAQ.

If nothing works, write to us (contact details on the home page).
