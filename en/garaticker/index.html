Garaticker, Canarian
stickers for your chats.

Free sticker packs made in the Canary Islands, by the studio and by guest artists.

Where to use them Are you a creator?

Where to use them.

The same packs, in the apps you already chat in. Animated ones appear wherever the platform supports them.

WhatsApp

Add the packs to WhatsApp from the Garaticker app, on Android and iPhone.

iMessage

On iPhone, they're right there in Messages through the app's extension.

Telegram and Signal

Every pack is also published on Telegram and Signal.

GIPHY and Instagram

The animated ones live on the Sanchesque channel on GIPHY, and from there in your Instagram Stories.

How do you add them? We walk you through it, app by app, in the Garaticker help.

How it works.

01

Made here

Packs by the studio and by guest artists, each with the name of whoever drew it and their own profile in the app.

02

Always up to date

New packs show up in the app without you having to update it.

03

Your ideas

Ask us for a theme or suggest a creator right from the app. Only the studio sees it.

Do you draw? Sign your own pack.

Garaticker is also a shop window for people creating in the islands. Your pack carries your name, you get your own profile in the app and you keep the rights to your work.
